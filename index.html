How Formnote works

Your camera and privacy

Video is processed on your device and not saved. Notes stay in this browser. The pose tracker loads from a public CDN.

Air pointer

Point with your index finger at five screen targets to calibrate. After calibration, only Start and Finish & save respond to pointing. Hold over a button until the dwell ring completes. Calibration stays in this browser; keep the same camera and sitting distance.

Camera framing

For sit-to-stand, set the camera at hip height, turn side-on, and show your hip, knee, and ankle. Use manual logging if needed.

Prototype limits

Angles are estimates, not clinical measurements. Follow your physio’s plan.
